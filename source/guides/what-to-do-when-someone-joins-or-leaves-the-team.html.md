# What to do when someone joins or leaves the team

There are a series of tasks to complete when someone joins or leaves the team. A delivery manager on the team is responsible for ensuring these tasks are completed. These tasks have been [documented internally(https://beisgov.sharepoint.com/_layouts/15/DocIdRedir.aspx?ID=QYURM3EF33H2-2014490825-12576).
